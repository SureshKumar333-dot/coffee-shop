Kaff - Cafe & Coffee Website

A modern, responsive coffee shop website designed to showcase products, location, opening hours, and a brand story. Perfect for a local café or coffee brand looking to establish an online presence.

## ✨ Live Demo

Check out the live site here: [https://sureshkumar333-dot.github.io/coffee-shop/](https://sureshkumar333-dot.github.io/coffee-shop/)

## 🚀 Features

- **Hero Section:** Bold headline "Best Coffee Great Company" with a welcoming message.
- **Brand Story:** "Roasted Coffee for Your Mood" section highlighting the café's philosophy.
- **Location & Assortment:** Displays café location and product variety.
- **Opening Hours:** Clear weekly schedule with Sunday closure notice.
- **Barista Party:** Promotional section for Friday & Sunday events.
- **Product Showcase:** Featured coffee beans with pricing (Peru Whole Bean).
- **Testimonials:** Customer reviews (placeholder content).
- **Blog Section:** Recent articles about coffee culture and industry news.
- **Responsive Design:** Fully responsive layout for all device sizes.

🎨 Customization
Content: Update text, product names, prices, and blog articles in index.html.

Images: Replace placeholder images in the assets/images/ folder.

Opening Hours: Modify schedule in the opening hours section.

Styling: Edit css/style.css to change colors, fonts, and layout.

Colors: Consider a warm, coffee-inspired color palette (browns, creams, dark greens).
